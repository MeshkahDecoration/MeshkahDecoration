## Meshkah Decoration

Handmade decor and gifts inspired by Islamic art and light, designed in Troy, Michigan.

- **Laser & Decor:** laser-cut wood and acrylic lanterns (fanoos), wall art and keychains
- **Print & Frame:** Islamic art prints, laminated and framed pieces
- **Apparel:** modest clothing, hijabs and scarves

We design every piece in our own studio, from the first sketch to the cut file.
